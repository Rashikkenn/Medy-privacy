MEDY PRIVACY POLICY

Effective 3 November 2026

Medy is a pill reminder app for Android, made by Rashik. This page explains what Medy does with your information.

Short version: everything stays on your phone. Medy has no account, no ads and no tracking, and it never sends your information anywhere.

What Medy stores

  • The pills you add: names, details, how many you take, times, first and last day, and pills in hand
  • Your history: which pills you marked Taken, Snoozed or Skipped
  • Your name, if you enter one
  • A pixel-art version of your photo, if you add one. The original photo is also kept on your phone, only so the art can be redone
  • Your settings

All of this is saved in Medy's own storage on your phone. Other apps cannot read it.

What Medy does not do

  • It does not connect to the internet. The app does not ask for internet permission.
  • It does not create accounts, show ads, track you or use analytics.
  • It does not share or sell anything, and it uses no third-party services.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show your reminders
  • Exact alarms: so reminders arrive on the minute
  • Run after restart: to set your reminders again when the phone restarts
  • Vibrate: for the reminder buzz

Google backup

If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may save a copy of Medy's data to your Google account and restore it on a new phone. That is controlled by your phone and Google, not by Medy. You can turn it off in your phone's settings.

Deleting your data

Open Medy, then Settings, then Help, then Start fresh to delete your pills, history, name and photo. Uninstalling Medy also removes everything on your phone. Copies made by Google backup are managed in your Google account.

Children

Medy is not made for children.

Not medical advice

Medy is a reminder, not a doctor. It does not give medical advice. For anything about your pills, ask your doctor or pharmacist.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
